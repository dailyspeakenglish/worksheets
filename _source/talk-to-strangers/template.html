8Sure or not sure? ↘ ↗ pronunciation6 points

Question tags are the secret of small talk. Your voice tells people what you mean.

↘ Voice goes DOWN
You're sure. You expect "yes". Perfect for small talk!
"Lovely day, isn't it? ↘"
↗ Voice goes UP
You're not sure. It's a real question.
"You work here, don't you? ↗"
A. Complete the tags.
1. Terrible weather, ?
2. You come here often, ?
3. It wasn't very busy yesterday, ?
B. Circle ↘ or ↗.
4. "Busy today, isn't it?" (You can see a long queue.) ↘ ↗
5. "You're Ana's flatmate, aren't you?" (You think so, but you're not sure.) ↘ ↗
6. "Lovely day, isn't it?" (The sun is shining.) ↘ ↗
Jack's tip: Say each sentence out loud twice: once going down ↘, once going up ↗. Can you hear how the meaning changes?
