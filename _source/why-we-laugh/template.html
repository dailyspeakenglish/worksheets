9Sincere or sarcastic? 😏 new! listening for meaning6 points

Read the situation. Does the person mean it (sincere), or mean the opposite (sarcastic)? Tick one. ⏱ 10:31

1. 🌧️ It's pouring with rain. Your friend says:

2. 🚗 Your friend has just passed her driving test. You say:
“Well done! I'm so proud of you!”
😊 SINCERE😏 SARCASTIC
3. 🥪 Your colleague drops his whole lunch on the floor. He says:
“Well, that went well.”
😊 SINCERE😏 SARCASTIC
4. 📰 Your colleague is relaxing with a coffee and a newspaper. Someone says:
“Wow, you look really busy!”
😊 SINCERE😏 SARCASTIC
5. ☀️ The sun is shining and it's warm. Your neighbour says:
“Lovely weather today!”
😊 SINCERE😏 SARCASTIC
6. 🍝 Your friend cooks you a delicious dinner. You say:
“This is amazing – thank you!”
😊 SINCERE😏 SARCASTIC
How to spot sarcasm: the words don't match the situation · the voice is slower, flatter or a bit exaggerated · there's often a tiny smile or a pause. Not sure? Just smile and say, "You're joking, right?" And be careful with sarcasm in messages – nobody can hear your voice!
