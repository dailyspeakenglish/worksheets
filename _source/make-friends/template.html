10Yes, no or another time? new! replying to invitations6 points

Someone invites you to something. Is each reply A (accept), D (decline politely) or S (suggest another time)?

✅ A = accept🙏 D = decline politely📅 S = suggest another time
1. “Count me in!”
2. “I'd love to, but I'm working on Saturday.”
3. “How about next Friday instead?”
4. “Sounds great – what time?”
5. “I'm afraid I can't this week. Thanks for asking!”
6. “What about a coffee on Sunday?”
Lucy's tip: A polite "no" = show interest + but + a reason: "Oh, I'd love to, but I'm visiting my mum." Add another time and the door stays open: "How about next week?" 🚪
