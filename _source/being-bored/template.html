⏱ Jack's two-minute rule
Feel the urge to grab your phone? Wait two minutes. Count your breaths, look out of the window – or describe what you see in English: "There's a woman with a red umbrella. The bus is late again." If the urge is still there, fine – now it's a choice, not an automatic movement.
Try it now! Describe one thing you can see:
Did you know? 🧸 Sandi Mann looked at children on long flights. Children with a whole bag of toys picked up each thing and moved on quickly. Children with one toy at a time stayed busy for much longer – and a plain notebook and a pen kept them happy for ages! Just like Jack's nephew and the box. 📦 ⏱ 8:40
